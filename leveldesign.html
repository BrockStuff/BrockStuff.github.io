I've been very focused on the Level Design sector of games as of late, meaning I've been able to quickly understand the process of conceptualising a level, using a toolset to produce a level, then testing it.

The process of creating a level

When designing a level, it starts with seeing what limitations I have, as well as what tools & items I can use. Those, as well as a pointer as to what my levels should be like, allow me to begin drawing out ideas for maps.

Strucken Town and its many, MANY revisions

Creating 'Strucken Town' was very difficult. As the level itself went through 4 different revisions before landing on the final map.

It's always important to me that I get the idea right, even if that means having many many ideas made, picture taken, then thrown out about a day or two later. So during the process of making these levels I made a blockout of the key parts of the level. From going building to another building, to a giant town-square-esque arena or the small and weird interiors. The important aspects were blocked out first, then their weird and wacky.
